# About Page Client Questionnaire — Verified Source Notes

## Source

- Source type: Client-completed About questionnaire
- Original language: Spanish
- Status: Verified client-provided information
- Purpose: Factual source material for the website About page

These notes preserve the client's facts separately from the polished English website copy. They are not intended to be published verbatim.

## Verified facts

- Company: Yucra's Home Improvement Services, LLC.
- The owner reported formally starting the business approximately one year before completing the 2026 questionnaire to establish his own company, a long-time goal. For durable website copy, the client directed this to be presented as the year 2025 rather than as a relative date.
- The owner reports more than 15 years of home-improvement industry experience. This is the owner's experience, not the age of the company.
- The owner enjoys many kinds of home-improvement work, with a particular interest in drywall and plaster work.
- The owner identifies treating customers well as a quality that differentiates the company.
- He approaches the work with dedication and good service and takes pride in the finished result.
- The owner specifically identified two completed full-basement projects as work he is proud of and reported that those customers were happy with the results.
- The owner says customers commonly report being happy and satisfied with his work. This is a general statement from the owner, not a direct customer testimonial.
- The described project process is: the customer makes contact; the owner prepares and sends an estimate; the customer accepts it; work begins; the project is approached with dedication; and the company works toward completion by the date agreed upon at the start.
- Free estimates are available.
- The owner's vision is to build a recognized company whose customers are satisfied with its work.
- The client does not want personal or family information included on the About page.
- The client's message to prospective customers is that they can feel confident putting their project in the company's hands.
